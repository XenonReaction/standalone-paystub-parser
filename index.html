Paystub Parser

A single-file, no-build-step version of this project's parser. Everything happens in this browser tab - nothing is uploaded anywhere, and nothing is saved once you close or reload this page.

$/hour paid

—

Net pay ÷ hours worked - what you actually took home, per hour.

$/hour, reimbursements removed

—

Same, but with mileage, GPS/data, and tips-reported entries stripped out entirely - just labor pay after taxes.

$/hour, mileage at federal rate

—

The reimbursements-removed rate, plus mileage recalculated at the federal rate above instead of the employer's.

These are this page's own estimates, not payroll or IRS figures. The federal mileage rate is a stand-in for typical vehicle costs, not this vehicle's actual cost.
