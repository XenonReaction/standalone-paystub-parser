Paystub Parser

A single-file, no-build-step version of this project's parser. Everything happens in this browser tab - nothing is uploaded anywhere, and nothing is saved once you close or reload this page.

$/hour apparent

—

Pay after federal taxes, plus the car reimbursement, ÷ hours worked - what the paycheck looks like per hour.

$/hour paid

—

Pay after federal taxes ÷ hours worked, with the car reimbursement left out.

$/hour after car costs

—

$/hour paid, minus the car costs the reimbursement didn't cover at the federal rate above.

These are this page's own estimates, not payroll or IRS figures. The federal mileage rate is a stand-in for typical vehicle costs, not this vehicle's actual cost.
